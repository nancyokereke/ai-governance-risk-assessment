# 90-Day AI Governance Roadmap

> **Simulation notice:** This is a simulated assessment based on fictional organisational details and assumed control gaps. It is not an actual security audit and contains no verified findings.

Part of the [AI Governance and Risk Assessment](../README.md) project. The complete report is available as a [PDF](../report/AI-Governance-Risk-Assessment.pdf).
| Timeline | Key activities | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1-30 | Appoint AI governance lead; publish acceptable-use policy; create approved tools register; introduce human review requirements; set up AI misuse reporting channel | Basic governance and accountability established |
| Days 31-60 | Deliver employee training; assess data protection controls; review priority AI vendors; introduce content verification; add SAST and dependency checks; add AI scenarios to incident response | Improved awareness and control coverage |
| Days 61-90 | Review implementation evidence; test selected controls and AI integrations; run tabletop exercise; reassess priority risks; update the risk register | Visibility into control effectiveness and residual risk |


### Proposed key performance indicators

- 100% of departments have documented AI use cases by day 30.
- Approved AI tools register established by day 30.
- Acceptable-use policy published and communicated by day 30.
- At least 95% of employees complete AI security awareness training by day 60, with the remainder completing by day 90.
- All externally published AI-assisted marketing content follows the review process.
- Priority AI vendors have documented security and privacy assessments by day 90.
- All High risks have assigned owners and documented treatment plans by day 30.

> These are proposed targets, not actual results.
