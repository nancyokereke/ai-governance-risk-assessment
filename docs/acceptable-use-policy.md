# AI Acceptable-Use Policy and Governance Structure

## AI Acceptable-Use Policy: Recommended Key Requirements

The organisation should introduce a formal AI acceptable-use policy with at least the following requirements.

1. **Approved tools.** Employees may only use AI tools approved by the organisation for business purposes.
2. **Data protection.** Employees must not submit confidential company information, personal data, customer records, credentials or sensitive business information to public or unapproved AI tools.
3. **Human oversight.** AI-generated outputs must be reviewed by an employee before being used for important decisions or published externally.
4. **Accuracy and verification.** Employees must independently verify facts, statistics, product claims and other important information generated by AI.
5. **Intellectual property.** Employees must consider copyright, licensing and IP requirements when using AI-generated content or code.
6. **Accountability.** Employees remain responsible for the accuracy, appropriateness and security of work produced with AI assistance.
7. **High-impact decisions.** AI must not make or finalise hiring, disciplinary or other high-impact decisions without documented human review.
8. **Integrations.** AI tools must not be connected to internal systems without security approval.
9. **Reporting.** Employees must report suspected data exposure, unauthorised AI use or other AI-related incidents through established channels.


## Recommended AI Governance Structure

AI governance should involve business leadership and operational teams, not only IT.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Approves AI governance direction and provides oversight |
| AI Governance Lead | Coordinates the programme and reports significant risks to leadership |
| IT/Security | Evaluates AI tools, manages security controls and monitors risks |
| Legal/Privacy | Reviews legal, privacy and contractual implications |
| HR | Coordinates awareness and training; oversees fair use in recruitment |
| Department Heads | Ensure responsible AI use within their teams |
| Employees | Follow AI policies and report potential issues |
